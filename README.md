## 🚀 About Me

I’m **Madan R**, a developer and electronics enthusiast focused on building **practical, connected, and intelligent systems**.

My work combines **web development with hardware and IoT**, allowing me to build projects that connect the physical world with modern digital interfaces.

I enjoy working across the complete system — from **Arduino and ESP-based hardware** to **web applications, dashboards, APIs, automation, and intelligent software**.

My interests include:

* 🌐 **Web Development** — responsive interfaces, dashboards & web applications
* 🔌 **IoT & Embedded Systems** — Arduino, ESP32 & ESP8266
* 🤖 **Automation & Intelligent Systems** — AI-assisted and automated solutions
* 📡 **Hardware Integration** — sensors, RFID, motors, communication modules & controllers
* ☁️ **Connected Applications** — Firebase, Google Apps Script & cloud-based systems
* 🛠️ **Project Development** — turning ideas into functional real-world prototypes
